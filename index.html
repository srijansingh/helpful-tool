Why use this instead of an upload-based converter?

Most free PDF tools work by uploading your file to a server, processing it there, and sending the result back. That's fine for most documents, but it means your file — which might have personal details, ID numbers, or anything else you'd rather not hand to a third party — leaves your device. This tool does the same merge, split and convert operations entirely inside your browser using open-source libraries (pdf-lib and pdf.js); your files never leave your computer.

Frequently asked questions

Is my file actually uploaded anywhere?

No. Every operation on this page runs in your browser's own JavaScript engine. There's no server call, no account, and nothing is stored once you close the tab.

Is there a file size or page limit?

No hard limit is enforced, but very large files are processed by your device's memory, not a server, so extremely large PDFs (hundreds of MB) may be slow or run out of memory on a low-end phone.

What page range formats does Split support?

Comma-separated numbers and ranges, e.g. 1-3,5,8 extracts pages 1, 2, 3, 5 and 8. Duplicate or out-of-range entries are ignored automatically.

Which image formats work for Images → PDF?

Any format your browser can display — JPG and PNG are kept at their original quality; other formats (WebP, GIF, BMP) are converted losslessly before being placed on the page.

Every conversion runs locally in your browser — nothing is uploaded, no account needed, no file is stored anywhere.
